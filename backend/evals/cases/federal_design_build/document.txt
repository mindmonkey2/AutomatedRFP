SOLICITATION NO. W000XX-26-R-0042
DESIGN-BUILD — VEHICLE MAINTENANCE FACILITY

SECTION L — INSTRUCTIONS, CONDITIONS, AND NOTICES TO OFFERORS

L.1 GENERAL
Offerors shall be registered in the System for Award Management (SAM) at the time of proposal submission and shall maintain registration until contract award.
This acquisition is a 100% Small Business Set-Aside under NAICS code 236220. Offerors must qualify as a small business under this NAICS code.
Proposals shall remain valid for 120 calendar days from the closing date of this solicitation.

L.2 SUBMISSION
Proposals are due no later than 1:00 p.m. Eastern Time on June 9, 2026. Proposals shall be submitted via the PIEE Solicitation Module; proposals submitted by email will not be accepted.
Questions must be submitted to the Contract Specialist by email no later than May 22, 2026. Questions received after this date may not be answered.
Offerors shall acknowledge all amendments by signing the SF 30 or by acknowledging them in block 19 of the SF 1442.
The Government intends to award without discussions but reserves the right to conduct discussions if determined necessary.

L.3 PROPOSAL ORGANIZATION
The proposal shall consist of four volumes:
Volume I — Price, including the completed SF 1442 and Bid Schedule.
Volume II — Technical Approach, not to exceed 30 pages.
Volume III — Past Performance, not to exceed 15 pages, excluding Past Performance Questionnaires.
Volume IV — Small Business Participation Plan, not to exceed 5 pages.
Each volume shall be submitted as a separate, searchable PDF file. Text shall be Times New Roman, 12 point, with one-inch margins. Pages exceeding the stated limits will not be read.

L.4 VOLUME II — TECHNICAL APPROACH
The Offeror shall describe its design-build team organization, including the lead designer of record, and the roles and responsibilities of each team member.
The Offeror shall provide a preliminary design narrative addressing the facility requirements in the Statement of Work, including sustainability features to achieve LEED Silver certification.
The Offeror shall provide a summary schedule in Gantt chart format showing design, permitting, construction, and commissioning, with completion within 540 calendar days after award.
The lead designer shall be a registered architect licensed in any U.S. state.

L.5 VOLUME III — PAST PERFORMANCE
Submit up to five (5) recent and relevant projects completed within the past six (6) years. A relevant project is a design-build or construction project of at least $10 million involving industrial, maintenance, or vehicle facilities.
For each project, the Offeror shall have the client complete the Past Performance Questionnaire (Attachment J-3) and return it directly to the Contracting Officer by the proposal due date.

L.6 VOLUME IV — SMALL BUSINESS PARTICIPATION
The Offeror shall describe the extent of participation of small businesses, including small disadvantaged, women-owned, HUBZone, and service-disabled veteran-owned small businesses, as a percentage of total contract value.

SECTION M — EVALUATION FACTORS FOR AWARD

M.1 BASIS FOR AWARD
Award will be made to the offeror whose proposal represents the best value to the Government, considering price and non-price factors.

M.2 EVALUATION FACTORS
Factor 1, Technical Approach, is more important than Factor 2, Past Performance. Factor 2 is more important than Factor 3, Small Business Participation. All non-price factors combined are approximately equal to price.
Past performance will be evaluated for recency, relevancy, and quality; offerors with no record of relevant past performance will receive a neutral rating.
Price will be evaluated for reasonableness; the Government may also evaluate price for balance.
