NORTHFIELD STATE UNIVERSITY — OFFICE OF FACILITIES PLANNING
REQUEST FOR QUALIFICATIONS: ARCHITECTURAL AND ENGINEERING SERVICES
Hartwell Science Building Renovation (Project No. NSU-2026-118)

This is a Qualifications-Based Selection; fees will be negotiated with the top-ranked firm after selection. Fee information must not be included in the submittal; submittals that include fees will be disqualified.

Statements of Qualifications are due by 3:00 p.m. Central Time on September 3, 2026.

Submit one (1) original and five (5) copies, plus one electronic copy on a USB drive, to the Office of Facilities Planning.

Submittals shall use Standard Form 330, Parts I and II. The SF 330 Part I, Section H, shall not exceed ten (10) pages.

Firms must be licensed to practice architecture in the State of Northfield. The engineer of record for each discipline must hold a Northfield professional engineering license.

The selected firm shall maintain professional liability insurance of at least $2,000,000 per claim.

[Page 2]
SUBMITTAL CONTENT

Section E: Provide resumes of key personnel, including the principal-in-charge, project architect, and lead mechanical, electrical, and plumbing engineers.

Section F: Provide up to ten example projects; at least three must be renovations of occupied laboratory or science buildings completed in the last eight years.

Section H: Describe your approach to phasing construction in an occupied building, including how you will maintain laboratory exhaust and life-safety systems during construction. Also describe your quality control process for construction documents.

Firms shall disclose any current or pending litigation related to professional services within the last five years.

Include a completed Disclosure of Lobbying Activities form (SF-LLL), if applicable.

EVALUATION
Submittals will be scored as follows:
Specialized experience in laboratory renovations (35%)
Qualifications of key personnel (25%)
Approach to occupied-building phasing (20%)
Past performance on schedule and budget (15%)
Familiarity with the University's design standards (5%)

The three highest-ranked firms will be invited to interview. The University will provide existing building drawings to the selected firm.
