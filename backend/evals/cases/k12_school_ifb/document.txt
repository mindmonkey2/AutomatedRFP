MAPLE VALLEY UNIFIED SCHOOL DISTRICT
NOTICE INVITING BIDS
Bid No. 2026-14 — New Classroom Building at Cedar Elementary School

Maple Valley Unified School District ("District") will receive sealed bids for Bid No. 2026-14, New Classroom Building at Cedar Elementary School ("Project"). The Project consists of a two-story, 18,000-square-foot classroom building, site utilities, and associated hardscape.

Sealed bids must be received at the District Facilities Office, 410 Orchard Way, Maple Valley, no later than 2:00 p.m. local time on Thursday, March 12, 2026, at which time they will be publicly opened and read aloud. Bids received after that time will be returned unopened.

A mandatory pre-bid conference and site walk will be held at 10:00 a.m. on February 24, 2026, at the Project site. Bidders who do not attend the mandatory pre-bid conference will be deemed non-responsive.

Each bidder shall possess a valid Class B (General Building Contractor) license issued by the State Contractors License Board at the time of bid submission.

Bidders and all listed subcontractors must be registered with the Department of Industrial Relations as public works contractors at the time of bid.

All questions regarding the bid documents must be submitted in writing to purchasing@maplevalleyusd.example no later than 5:00 p.m. on March 3, 2026. The District will issue any clarifications as written addenda posted on the District website.

The District reserves the right to reject any or all bids and to waive any irregularities in any bid.

INSTRUCTIONS TO BIDDERS

1. Bid Form. Bids shall be submitted on the Bid Form included in these documents. All blanks on the Bid Form shall be completed in ink or typewritten.

2. Bid Security. Each bid must be accompanied by bid security in the form of a cashier's check, certified check, or bid bond in an amount not less than ten percent (10%) of the total bid amount, payable to the District.

3. Bid Bond Surety. Any bid bond shall be issued by an admitted surety insurer authorized to do business in the State.

4. Required Forms. The following forms shall be completed, signed, and submitted with the bid:
a. Designation of Subcontractors
b. Non-Collusion Declaration
c. Bidder's Certificate Regarding Workers' Compensation
d. Iran Contracting Act Certification

5. Acknowledgment of Addenda. Bidders shall acknowledge receipt of all addenda on the Bid Form. Failure to acknowledge any addendum may result in rejection of the bid.

6. Experience. Bidders shall have completed at least two (2) public school construction projects of $5,000,000 or more within the last seven (7) years, and shall list those projects on the Bidder's Qualification Statement.

7. Withdrawal of Bids. A bid may be withdrawn by written request received by the District before the bid deadline.

[Page 3]
8. Contract Bonds. The successful bidder shall furnish a Performance Bond and a Payment Bond, each in the amount of one hundred percent (100%) of the contract price, within ten (10) calendar days after notice of award.

9. Insurance. The successful bidder shall provide certificates of insurance showing commercial general liability coverage of not less than $2,000,000 per occurrence and $4,000,000 aggregate.

10. Contract Time. The Contractor shall achieve substantial completion within 420 calendar days from the date of the Notice to Proceed.

11. Bid Validity. Bids shall remain valid for sixty (60) days after the bid opening.

12. Disabled Veteran Business Enterprise (DVBE). The District has a DVBE participation goal of three percent (3%). Bidders shall submit the DVBE Participation Form with the bid, documenting either attainment of the goal or good-faith efforts.

13. Award. The District will award the contract to the lowest responsive, responsible bidder.
