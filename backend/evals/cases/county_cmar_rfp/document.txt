RIVERBEND COUNTY
REQUEST FOR PROPOSALS 26-031
Construction Manager at Risk (CMAR) Services — Riverbend County Public Safety Building

1. INTRODUCTION
Riverbend County ("County") seeks proposals from qualified firms to provide preconstruction and construction services as Construction Manager at Risk for a new 85,000-square-foot Public Safety Building with an estimated construction cost of $48 million.

2. SCHEDULE
RFP issued: April 6, 2026
Deadline for written questions: April 17, 2026, 4:00 p.m.
Proposals due: May 1, 2026, 2:00 p.m.
Shortlisted firm interviews: week of May 18, 2026

3. SUBMISSION REQUIREMENTS
3.1 Proposals must be submitted electronically through the County's eProcurement portal as a single PDF file. Hard-copy or emailed proposals will not be accepted.
3.2 Proposals shall not exceed twenty-five (25) pages, excluding the cover letter, resumes, and required forms. Pages beyond this limit will not be evaluated.
3.3 Pages shall be 8.5 x 11 inches with a minimum font size of 11 points.
3.4 Proposals shall be organized in the order of the tabs in Section 4, with tabs labeled to match.

[Page 2]
4. PROPOSAL CONTENT

Tab A — Cover Letter. Provide a cover letter signed by an officer authorized to bind the firm. The cover letter shall not exceed two (2) pages.

Tab B — Firm Qualifications. Describe your firm's experience as CMAR on at least three public safety, justice, or comparable civic facilities completed in the last ten years, including project cost, delivery method, and owner reference contact.

Tab C — Key Personnel. Identify the proposed project executive, project manager, superintendent, and preconstruction manager. Include a one-page resume for each. Key personnel identified in the proposal may not be substituted without the County's written approval.

Tab D — Preconstruction Approach. Describe your approach to estimating, value engineering, constructability review, and developing the Guaranteed Maximum Price (GMP).

Tab E — Safety. Provide your firm's Experience Modification Rate (EMR) for each of the last three years. Describe your site safety program, including subcontractor safety oversight.

Tab F — Fee Proposal. Submit your proposed preconstruction fee as a lump sum and your construction-phase fee as a percentage of the cost of work, using Attachment B, Fee Proposal Form, uploaded as a separate file.

Tab G — Required Forms. Include the signed Conflict of Interest Disclosure (Attachment C) and the Vendor Registration Certificate (Attachment D).

[Page 3]
5. MINIMUM QUALIFICATIONS
Proposers must hold a current general contractor license issued by the State. Proposers must demonstrate bonding capacity of at least $60 million for a single project, evidenced by a letter from a surety. Proposals that do not meet the minimum qualifications will be rejected without further evaluation.

6. EVALUATION CRITERIA
Proposals will be evaluated by a selection committee using the following criteria:
Firm qualifications and relevant experience — 30 points
Key personnel — 25 points
Preconstruction approach — 20 points
Safety record — 10 points
Fee proposal — 15 points

7. COUNTY RESPONSIBILITIES
The County shall provide the selected firm with design documents at each design phase for review and cost estimating.

8. RESERVATION OF RIGHTS
The County may, at its sole discretion, cancel this RFP or reject any or all proposals.
