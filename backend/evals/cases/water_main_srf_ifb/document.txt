CITY OF WESTBROOK
INVITATION FOR BIDS No. PW-26-07
Water Main Replacement, Phase 3

Sealed bids are due at 11:00 a.m. on July 15, 2026, at the City Clerk's Office, 200 Main Street, Westbrook.

FUNDING REQUIREMENTS
This project is funded in part by the State Drinking Water State Revolving Fund (DWSRF). The following federal requirements apply:

• American Iron and Steel: all iron and steel products used in the project must be produced in the United States. Bidders shall submit the AIS Certification with the bid.

• Davis-Bacon: Contractors and subcontractors must pay wages not less than the Davis-Bacon prevailing wage rates included in Appendix D.

• Disadvantaged Business Enterprise: Bidders shall make good faith efforts to meet the DBE fair share objectives of 4% MBE and 6% WBE, and shall submit EPA Forms 6100-3 and 6100-4 with the bid.

• Debarment: Bidders must not be suspended or debarred from federal programs, and shall submit the Certification Regarding Debarment (Attachment E).

BID REQUIREMENTS

Bidders shall submit unit prices for every line item on the Bid Schedule. Bids with missing unit prices will be considered non-responsive. In case of a discrepancy between a unit price and the extended total, the unit price will govern.

A bid bond of five percent (5%) of the total base bid is required.

Bidders must be prequalified with the Westbrook Public Works Department in the Underground Utilities category prior to bid opening.

The bidder shall have at least five (5) years of experience installing ductile iron water mains of 8-inch diameter or larger, and shall list three (3) such projects with references on the Statement of Experience form.

All work shall be completed within 180 calendar days of the Notice to Proceed. Liquidated damages of $1,500 per calendar day will be assessed for each day beyond the contract time.

A Traffic Control Plan prepared by a certified traffic control supervisor shall be submitted to the City Engineer for approval at least 10 days before starting work.

The City will provide water for testing and flushing at no charge.
